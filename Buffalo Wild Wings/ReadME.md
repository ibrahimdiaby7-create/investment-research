# Buffalo Wild Wings LBO

This model analyses Roark Capital’s 2017 acquisition of Buffalo Wild Wings through a five-year leveraged buyout framework.

## Transaction Overview
- Entry Enterprise Value: **~$2.6bn**
- LTM EBITDA: **~$289m**
- Entry Multiple: **~9.1x EBITDA**
- Sponsor Equity: **~$699m**

## Investment Case
The model compares Buffalo Wild Wings’ management projections against a sponsor case focused on operational improvement, refranchising and deleveraging.

The sponsor case assumes revenue declines as company-owned restaurants are refranchised, while improved margins and a more asset-light business model drive EBITDA growth. Cash proceeds from franchise-unit sales and operating free cash flow are used to reduce acquisition debt.

## Key Findings
- EBITDA increases from approximately **$301m to $419m**
- Exit multiple held at approximately **9.1x**, avoiding reliance on multiple expansion
- Exit Enterprise Value: **~$3.8bn**
- Exit Sponsor Equity Value: **~$2.2bn**
- **MOIC: ~3.1x**
- **5-Year IRR: ~25%**
